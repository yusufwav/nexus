Why this exists

Built to bring you up to the level the world is already working at

Nexus exists for one reason: to get first year Computer Science students at Nelson Mandela University on par with the best in the world, starting with the modules you're taking right now. And here's the honest truth nobody in academia wants to say out loud: most people today simply don't have the attention span to learn from a textbook, and building a course around the assumption that they do is why so many capable students fall behind.

  1. The notes you get on campus are sufficient. They really are. But sufficient isn't the same as understanding, so I took them apart and rebuilt them: clearer structure, worked examples, and the reasoning behind every step instead of the steps alone.
  2. AI fluency is quietly becoming an unofficial job requirement, and the industry isn't going to wait for universities to offer a qualification in it. Learning to work with these tools now, while you're still studying, is the difference between catching up later and leading from the start.
  3. Everything here is tuned toward one outcome: making you genuinely competitive with students anywhere in the world, at a price a student can actually afford. No gatekeeping, no fluff, just the tools and the understanding to use them.
  4. For some students, learning just clicks. For the rest of us, it takes hearing the same idea a different way, in our own words, at our own pace. That's what the AI study partner is for: it learns how you learn, right as you learn it.

A quick word about me: I'm a first year Computer Science student at NMU, building this in my own time because I remember exactly how disorienting that first semester is. I'm not a company. I'm one person who figured some of this out the hard way and would rather hand you the shortcut than watch you struggle. If the notes help you, wonderful. If something is unclear or wrong, tell me and I'll fix it. That's the deal.
